1. Information We Collect

When you use Fetchreel, we may collect or process the following categories of information:

  • When you submit a video URL through our Service, the URL may be processed by our systems in order to provide the requested functionality.
  • We may automatically receive limited technical information, which may include: IP address; Browser type and version
  • We may collect limited information about how the Service is used, such as the frequency of requests, performance information, and error information. This information may be used to maintain and improve the Service.

Fetchreel does not require users to create an account or provide payment information in order to use the basic Service. We do not intentionally request government identification documents or other identity documents through the Service.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information collected through the Service for the following purposes:

  • To process video URLs and provide the requested functionality;
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Service;
  • To protect the Service against abuse, spam, fraud, and malicious activity.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. We will not use information for unrelated purposes unless permitted or required by applicable law.

3. Data Storage and Retention

When you request a download, video files may be temporarily processed and stored on our servers or infrastructure necessary to provide the requested Service. Temporary files are intended to be automatically deleted after processing or within a reasonable predefined retention period. We do not intend to permanently retain submitted video URLs for ordinary Service operation. However, certain technical information, logs, or security records may be retained for a limited period where reasonably necessary for security, troubleshooting, legal compliance, or preventing abuse. Actual retention periods may vary depending on technical, operational, and legal requirements.

4. Third-party sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may disclose or provide limited information to third parties in the following circumstances:

  • We may disclose information where we reasonably believe disclosure is required or permitted by applicable law, regulation, legal process, court order, or valid governmental request.
  • We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, abuse, security incidents, violations of our Terms of Service, or threats to the rights, safety, or security of Fetchreel, our users, or others.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

Fetchreel may use limited cookies, browser storage, or similar technologies to support basic website functionality. These technologies may be used for purposes such as: Remembering basic preferences; Maintaining temporary session or application state; Improving website functionality; and Supporting security and technical operations. We do not intentionally use invasive tracking technologies for unrelated purposes. If third-party analytics, advertising, or other services are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated to describe the relevant technologies and their purposes. You can generally manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain storage technologies may affect some functionality of the Service.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information.

  • The right to request information about personal data we may hold about you;
  • The right to request deletion of personal information, subject to applicable legal exceptions;
  • The right to stop using the Service at any time.

8. Contact

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us through our Contact Page. We will review privacy-related inquiries and respond as appropriate.
